Cookie Policy

This cookie policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on the website www.mantenimientosnl.com, and how you can manage them. By continuing to browse our website, you accept the use of cookies as described in this document.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet, mobile) when you visit them. They allow the site to remember your preferences and actions over time, so you do not have to re-enter certain information on every visit.

In addition to cookies in the strict sense, this website also uses local storage (localStorage), a similar technology that saves information in your browser without an automatic expiry date.

2. Cookies we use

This website is a static service-presentation site. It does not use analytics or advertising systems. The only storage technologies employed are as follows:

Name Type Purpose Duration Provider
cookieConsent localStorage Stores your choice to accept or reject cookies so the banner is not shown again Persistent (until browser data is cleared) Mantenimientos NL (own)

3. Third-party cookies

This website loads external resources for its correct visual functioning:

  • Google Fonts — web typefaces loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may log the font request; see their policy at policies.google.com.
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — vector icons. Cloudflare may log resource requests.
  • AOS (unpkg.com) — scroll-animation library. No own cookies.

None of these services place tracking or advertising cookies on your device through our website.

4. How to manage or delete cookies

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing the local storage data in your browser. Below you will find instructions for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Menu → Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Menu → Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.
  • Microsoft Edge: Menu → Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data → Cookies.

Please note that clearing this data will cause the cookie banner to reappear on your next visit.

5. Legal basis

The storage of the cookie preference (cookieConsent) is based on the legitimate interest of the website owner in not repeatedly displaying the consent banner once the user has already made a choice, pursuant to Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and the applicable Spanish ePrivacy regulations.
